# Red-flag checklist

Score each flag HIT or CLEAR with evidence. 0–1 flags: CLEAN. 2–3: CAUTION. 4+: LIKELY SCAM.

## The flags

1. **Pay-to-play economics** — You must deposit or spend more than your realistic share of the prize. The killer version: total player deposits dwarf the prize pool. (Steve Arena: ~60 players × ~$200 = ~$12,000 locked up chasing $500 in prizes.)
2. **Someone else holds your money** — Deposits go into a wallet, contract, or account you don't control, with no audited escrow and no clear withdrawal path.
3. **Subjective judging** — Winners picked on vibes ("creative use," "community fit") instead of verifiable metrics (PnL, on-chain proof, timestamps).
4. **Urgency pressure** — Countdown timers, "deadline tonight," limited spots — engineered to stop you doing the math.
5. **Vague or shifting terms** — Prize amounts, judging criteria, payout dates, or fee structures that are fuzzy, change between rounds, or live only in a chat message.
6. **No proof of past payouts** — Claims of previous winners with no on-chain transactions, no named winners, no verifiable history.
7. **Reputation laundering** — A legit platform or a known name adjacent to an unknown sponsor. The platform's reputation is not the sponsor's. Vet them separately.
8. **Sunk-cost hooks** — "You've already deposited — just a little more to qualify / unlock / boost." Each step is small; the total isn't.
9. **Unverifiable claims** — "Backed by $1B," "audited," "thousands of users" with no link, no report, no numbers you can check.
10. **Credential or access harvesting** — Requires sign-in via OAuth, wallet connection, or permissions the task doesn't need. The "opportunity" may be the excuse; your access is the product.

## Golden rules (apply even when flags are few)

- **Never deposit into a wallet you don't control for a prize smaller than your deposit.**
- **Expected value first:** (your realistic win chance × prize) − cost to enter. If it's negative, it's entertainment, not income — price it like entertainment.
- **A real sponsor pays winners from their own pocket.** If the prize pool is funded by player deposits, it's a lottery wearing a costume.

## Case file: Steve Agent Arena (2026-10-01)

- Promise: 500 USDC prize pool (250/150/100), "launch your AI agent."
- Cost: sign in with Google/X (human), post from your X account (human), fund ~50 USDC + ~1 SOL (~$200+) into their arena wallet, minimum 5 qualifying trades.
- Flags hit: #1 (pay-to-play: $12K deposits vs $500 prizes), #2 (their wallet holds your funds), #3 (judging: strategy 35%, "creative use" 30%, activity 20%, content 15% — subjective), #4 (deadline tonight), #7 (legit Superteam platform, separate unknown sponsor OOBE).
- Score: 5 of 10 → **LIKELY SCAM** (bad-economics variant, not an outright rug).
- Verdict: walk away. The math doesn't work at any skill level.
